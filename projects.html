Voronoi Hash Encoding

I am exploring Voronoi-based hashing as an alternative to regular multi-resolution hash grids for neural fields. The goal is to use adaptive Voronoi or power-diagram cells, together with their Delaunay dual, to obtain a continuous barycentric encoding that concentrates capacity near geometric detail while avoiding dense uniform grids.

Beyond Infinity

I developed To Infinity and Beyond, a GPU-driven synthetic data pipeline based on Unreal Engine 5. The system keeps rendering, memory sharing, processing, and neural-network training on the GPU, reducing CPU-GPU transfer bottlenecks and enabling real-time generation of virtually unlimited training data from interactive 3D environments.

Backpropagation-free Learning

I am investigating learning algorithms that reduce or remove the dependence on standard backpropagation, allowing to reduce computational overhead and explore alternative learning paradigms. My experiments focus on biologically plausible credit assignment, local learning rules, and brain-inspired architectures such as Agglomerator.

World-scale Digital Twins

I am building world-scale digital twin pipelines in Unreal Engine, using real geospatial data, synthetic rendering, and simulation. The work targets real-time graphics-driven training and validation, from UAV trajectories and urban reconstruction to radio-propagation experiments with ray-tracing and synthetic-to-real computer vision benchmarks.
